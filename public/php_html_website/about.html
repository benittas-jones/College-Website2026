Legacy Since 1997

A Brief History of C.S.I. Ewart Women’s Christian College

The Madras Diocese of the Church of South India founded the C.S.I. Ewart Women’s Christian College in 1997. Rev. Sandy MacDonald, Moderator of the Church of Scotland declared the College open on 29th September 1997.

The Rt. Rev. Dr. Masilamani Azariah, Bishop of Madras inaugurated the College in its first location at C.S.I. Ewart Girl’s Higher Secondary School, Purasawalkam on 31st October 1997. Dr. R. Premila Chandrasekaran was the first Principal. Mrs. Ahalya Williams served as Vice-Principal. 11 faculty, 10 non-teaching staff members and 29 students were the pioneering group to form the College. Dr. S. Augustine Chellappa and Prof. S. David served as Correspondent of the College in the early years.

Move to Melrosapuram (June 2001)

Bishop M. Azariah was inspired to establish the College to provide higher education for rural young women, especially from the villages in Madras Diocese. His vision was to embody inclusive, accessible education to those who need to access the opportunities.

Ewart College moved to Melrosapuram in June 2001 with 11 acres of open space to enjoy. The beautiful spacious College buildings were designed by Architect Jayakar and funded mainly by Madras Diocesan Schools, C.S.I. Melrosapuram High School and the C.S.I. Rural Community College. The College offers Higher Education that transforms the lives of the people.

Rev. Adam Andrew & 1893 Historical Association

Melrosapuram has a long history of association with education. This village was established as a Christian settlement in 1893 by the Church of Scotland missionary Rev. Adam Andrew. Andrew established a school to teach young students from several villages and also offered skills development training in the vast and productive agricultural field. Education was dear to Andrew and his wife Elizabeth so they established many village schools for boys and girls. They knew that education was the most important avenue of progress for the rural and poor students, especially for girl students.

PG Expansion & Centres

The Internal Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC), since its inception in 2015 acts as a nodal unit of the Institution. The College has moved to become a Post Graduate institution since 2019 with the addition of BA and MA in English Literature and M.Com.

The College has also instituted: Centre for Women's Studies, Centre for Human Resource Development, Center for Career Advancement, Centre for Innovation, Center for Development and Research, and Entrepreneurship Development. Elizabeth Andrew Hostel was started in 2005.

Foundational Purpose

College Mission Statement

C.S.I. Ewart Women’s Christian College is founded on faith in the Triune God and draws its inspiration from the Bible and the life and example of Jesus Christ for its ministry of education. Nurtured by eternal Christian values, the College aims to train young women to be serving, caring and sharing citizens of our great land and the world at large.

Priority & Liberation of Women

The priority of the College is the education of poor, oppressed and less privileged women, especially village women, to provide them opportunities for a fuller life. The College is committed to the liberation of women and training them to meet all challenges of gender and other forms of discrimination.

The College community as a whole will be a new ‘humanized’ society where learning will transform, sensitize and inspire everyone to challenge the structures that divide people, and where truth will set free people from inhuman and superstitious beliefs.

Academic Standards Commitment

The College believes that its commitment to these values is fully compatible with the maintenance of the highest academic standards and offering of courses and programs appropriate to the current needs of the society and the aspirations of the students.

Continuous Improvement

Quality Policy

C.S.I. Ewart Women's Christian College commits to imparting value-based quality education to women, augmenting experiential learning through state-of-the-art facilities, continuous faculty development, and regular IQAC academic audits.

Christian Foundation

Faith and Heritage

Administered by the CSI Diocese of Madras, spiritual life is central to our ethos. Daily morning devotion, chapel services, and the college motto “Love and Serve” mold young women into principled leaders for church and society.

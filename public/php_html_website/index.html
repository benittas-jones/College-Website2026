Founder & His Vision

Nurtured by Daring Faith & Hard Industry

“Faith is the assurance of things hoped for, the conviction of things not seen.” — Hebrews 11:1

Ideas and dreams begin small as a mustard seed but they grow to great heights and proportions, when nurtured by daring faith and hard industry.

The Ewart Women’s Christian College is one such dream that was born into existence through faith, nurtured by the Church of South India and the good will of many.

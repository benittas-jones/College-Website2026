Examination Rules

Instructions to Candidates

  • Candidates must produce their valid Hall Ticket and College Identity Card on each session of the examination.
  • Students are strictly advised to occupy their allotted seats 15 minutes prior to the commencement of the exam.
  • No candidate will be allowed into the exam hall after 30 minutes from the commencement of the exam.

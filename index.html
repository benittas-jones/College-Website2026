Heritage & Higher Purpose

Driven by Faith, Nurtured by Service

The village of Melrosapuram carries a rich educational tradition dating back to 1893, founded by Church of Scotland missionary Rev. Adam Andrew and his wife Elizabeth to bring schooling and agricultural skill-building to rural children.

Carrying forward this sacred trust, Bishop Dr. Masilamani Azariah envisioned C.S.I. Ewart Women's Christian College as an instrument of liberation. The College has instituted 6 specialized student centres to inspire research and empower young women:

Centre for Women's Studies

Sensitizing on gender equality and rural leadership.

Centre for Human Resource Dev.

Cultivating managerial & soft-skill competencies.

Centre for Career Advancement

Placement training and competitive exam coaching.

Centre for Innovation

Student project incubation and technology exhibits.

Center for Dev. & Research (CDR)

Fostering academic publications and social surveys.

Entrepreneurship Development

Mentoring women to launch self-sustaining enterprises.

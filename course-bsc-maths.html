Programme Overview

The B.Sc. Mathematics programme cultivates analytical sharpness, deductive reasoning, and problem-solving mastery. Spanning classical algebra, real and complex analysis, differential equations, mechanics, and numerical methods, this degree builds the intellectual foundation needed for competitive examinations (TNPSC, UPSC, Banking) as well as data-driven careers and advanced postgraduate degrees (M.Sc., MCA, MBA).

Eligibility Criteria

Candidates must have passed 10+2 / Higher Secondary Examinations (TN State Board, CBSE, ICSE or equivalent) with Mathematics as one of the mandatory core subjects.

Affiliation & CBCS Framework

All undergraduate and postgraduate programmes at C.S.I. Ewart Women's Christian College adhere to the curriculum and credit structure prescribed by the University of Madras under the Choice-Based Credit System (CBCS). Students earn core course credits, non-major electives, skill-based certificates, value education, and extension service credits (NSS, YRC, RRC).

Programme Overview

The B.Sc. Home Science Clinical Nutrition and Dietetics programme prepares students for health, dietary planning, and medical nutrition care. Through scientifically structured coursework, laboratory investigations, and hospital internships, students learn how nutrition drives human wellness, disease recovery, and community health.

Graduates pursue rewarding careers as Registered Dietitians, Hospital Nutritionists, Food Quality Officers, Public Health Consultants, and Wellness Entrepreneurs.

Eligibility Criteria

Candidates must have passed 10+2 / Higher Secondary Examinations (TN State Board, CBSE, ICSE or equivalent) with Chemistry and Biology / Botany / Zoology or Home Science as subjects of study.

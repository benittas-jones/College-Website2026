How to Reach

Public Transport & Connectivity

By Suburban Train: Singaperumal Koil Railway Station (5 km) on Chennai Beach – Chengalpattu line. Frequent share-autos and college buses operate to the campus.

By Bus (MTC / TNSTC): Accessible from GST Road through Singaperumal Koil Bus Stop or Melrosapuram Koot Road.

College Bus Fleet: Operating across Maraimalai Nagar, Guduvanchery, Chengalpattu town, Tambaram, and surrounding rural communities.

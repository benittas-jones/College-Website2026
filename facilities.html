Campus Infrastructure & Facilities

11-Acre Lush Green Campus • Designed by Architect Jayakar

IT Infrastructure

High-Tech Computer Labs

Modern air-conditioned computer laboratories equipped with high-speed internet, networked workstations, programming environments, and database suites.

Knowledge Centre

Central Library & e-Resources

Extensive collection of text books, reference journals, DELNET subscriptions, digital magazines, and peaceful study carrels for research scholars.

Events & Symposia

Auditorium & Seminar Halls

Spacious multimedia-enabled halls for diocesan convocations, national symposiums, guest lectures, cultural fests, and chapel services.

Practical Learning

Departmental Laboratories

Well-maintained apparatus, safety systems, and demonstration equipment supporting experimental curriculum and hands-on scientific inquiries.

Physical Wellbeing

Sports & Athletics Arena

Expansive open playground on the 11-acre campus supporting Track & Field, Volleyball, Kho-Kho, Throwball, and indoor Table Tennis and Chess.

Student Welfare

Transport & Nutritious Canteen

Dedicated fleet of college buses connecting Chengalpattu, Singaperumal Koil, Tambaram, and surrounding villages, alongside a hygienic canteen.

Eligibility Criteria

Eligibility & Admission Guidelines

  • UG Degree Courses: Passed +2 / Higher Secondary Examination from Tamil Nadu State Board, CBSE, ICSE or equivalent recognized board.
  • B.Sc Computer Science / BCA: Mathematics / Business Maths / Computer Science at +2 level preferred.
  • B.Com / BBA: Commerce / Accountancy / Business Studies stream in Higher Secondary.
  • PG Degree Courses: Recognized Bachelor's Degree in the relevant discipline.
  • Scholarships: Government SC/ST/OBC scholarships and Diocesan fee waivers available for rural deserving women.
